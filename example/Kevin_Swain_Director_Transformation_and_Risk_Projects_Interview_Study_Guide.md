## 7. High-yield behavioural & situational mock questions with model answer outlines
### Question 1:
- **Question:** "Can you describe a time you led a significant change initiative?"
- **What the panel is testing:** Ability to lead change and manage resistance.
- **Core response architecture & talking points:** Use STAR format to describe the situation, actions taken, and results achieved.
- **Follow-up probes to expect:** "What challenges did you face?" "How did you measure success?"

### Question 2:
- **Question:** "How do you approach risk management in your projects?"
- **What the panel is testing:** Understanding of risk management principles.
- **Core response architecture & talking points:** Discuss previous experiences with risk assessment and mitigation strategies.
- **Follow-up probes to expect:** "Can you provide a specific example?" "How do you prioritize risks?"

### Question 3:
- **Question:** "Describe a situation where you had to manage conflicting stakeholder interests."
- **What the panel is testing:** Conflict resolution and negotiation skills.
- **Core response architecture & talking points:** Use a relevant story to illustrate your approach to stakeholder management.
- **Follow-up probes to expect:** "What was the outcome?" "How did you ensure all voices were heard?"

### Question 4:
- **Question:** "What strategies do you use to ensure compliance with regulations?"
- **What the panel is testing:** Knowledge of compliance and regulatory frameworks.
- **Core response architecture & talking points:** Discuss your understanding of relevant regulations and your approach to ensuring compliance.
- **Follow-up probes to expect:** "How do you stay updated on regulatory changes?" "Can you give an example of a compliance challenge?"

### Question 5:
- **Question:** "How do you measure the success of your initiatives?"
- **What the panel is testing:** Ability to evaluate and report on performance.
- **Core response architecture & talking points:** Discuss the use of KPIs and metrics in assessing success.
- **Follow-up probes to expect:** "What specific metrics have you used?" "How do you communicate results to stakeholders?"

### Question 6:
- **Question:** "Can you provide an example of how you fostered a culture of continuous improvement?"
- **What the panel is testing:** Commitment to quality improvement and innovation.
- **Core response architecture & talking points:** Share a story that highlights your efforts in promoting continuous improvement.
- **Follow-up probes to expect:** "What challenges did you encounter?" "How did you engage your team?"
