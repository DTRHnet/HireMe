About The Royal

As one of Canada’s foremost mental health care and academic health science centres, The Royal has a clear purpose: to get more people living with mental illness into recovery faster. This is at the core of everything we do and it is driven by the passion, focus and dedication of our employees. Every day, the work that we do transforms the lives of people with mental illness through specialized mental healthcare, advocacy, research and education.

About the Program

Reporting to the Chief Operating Officer, the Director, Transformation and Risk Management provides strategic leadership for enterprise transformation, organizational planning, risk management, compliance, emergency preparedness, and corporate initiatives across The Royal.

 

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Planning & Organizational Transformation

    Lead enterprise strategic planning, organizational performance, and transformation initiatives aligned with the hospital's strategic plan.
    Advise senior leadership using data, stakeholder input, and industry insights to support strategic decision-making.
    Champion change management, innovation, continuous improvement, and organizational learning.
    Build partnerships with healthcare, government, academic, and community stakeholders.
    Prepare strategic recommendations and reports for the Executive Leadership Team, Board Committees and Board of Directors.

Risk Management Leadership

    Lead and continuously improve the Enterprise Risk Management framework.
    Oversee risk identification, assessment, mitigation, and reporting across clinical, operational, financial, labour relations, and technology domains.
    Ensure compliance with legislation, regulatory requirements, accreditation standards, hospital policies, and collective agreements.
    Partner with Legal Services, HR, Labour Relations, and technology leaders to strengthen governance, policy management, privacy, and compliance.

Emergency Management

    Provide strategic oversight of emergency preparedness, response, recovery, and mitigation activities.
    Ensure compliance with Accreditation Canada standards and applicable legislation.
    Lead organizational readiness activities, emergency exercises, and external emergency management partnerships.
    Oversee hazard identification and risk assessment (HIRA) activities and organizational readiness programs 
    Support Incident Management System (IMS) and Incident Command System (ICS) readiness and leadership development. 

Portfolio Financial Stewardship & Human Resources Management

    Lead portfolio planning, budgeting, financial stewardship, and sustainability initiatives.
    Develop business cases, cost-benefit analyses, and ROI evaluations to support strategic investments.
    Lead, mentor, and develop staff while supporting workforce planning, recruitment, and performance management.

Epic Program Sustainability

    Lead post-go-live Epic governance, optimization, and long-term sustainability.
    Monitor Epic utilization and adoption metrics and prioritize enhancement requests.
    Ensure Epic capabilities are fully leveraged, including Key Performance Indicators and system reliability.

Qualifications

    Master's degree in a relevant discipline (e.g., Master of Health Administration (MHA), Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Public Health (MPH).
    10+ years of progressive leadership experience in healthcare transformation, risk management, or operational leadership within a hospital or healthcare system in Ontario.
    Demonstrated experience leading complex, multi-stakeholder change initiatives with measurable impact.
    Preferred:  Certified Healthcare Executive (CHE), Professional designations e.g., Project Management Professional (PMP), Canadian Risk Management (CRM) or Prosci Change Management Certification (PROSCI)

Knowledge and Skills

    In-depth knowledge of Ontario's healthcare regulatory environment, funding models, risk governance, and quality improvement methodologies.
    Strong analytical, problem-solving, and strategic thinking skills in a complex healthcare environment.
    Proven ability to influence and collaborate with executive leaders, clinicians, union representatives, and healthcare partners.
    Advanced financial and business acumen, including business case development, budgeting, and cost-benefit analysis.
    Demonstrated experience leading large-scale transformation, project, and change management initiatives.

Language Requirements
